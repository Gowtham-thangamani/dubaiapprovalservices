What is Civil Defense?

Founded in 1976, the Dubai Civil Defense (DCD) is a key organization responsible for managing emergency situations across Dubai. It grants the essential Dubai Civil Defense Approval for various services, ensuring safety and compliance. With its mission to make Dubai one of the safest and most secure cities in the world, DCD, under the leadership of Major General Rashid Thani Al Matroushi, has played a vital role in saving lives during emergencies. The organization comprises several specialized departments, including DAFRA, DASPT, and DAA, all working in unison to protect both the city and its residents.

Why Choose Civil Defense?

Dubai Civil Defense has been instrumental in ensuring the highest standards of safety and security across industrial facilities, factories, and commercial buildings throughout Dubai. In addition to its role in safeguarding properties, it also manages critical rescue operations during various emergency situations. The primary goal of Dubai Civil Defense is to protect public lives and property. Frequently Asked Questions

What is Dubai Civil Defense (DCD) approval?

Dubai Civil Defense approval certifies that a building's fire and life-safety systems comply with the UAE Fire and Life Safety Code. It is mandatory for fire alarm, firefighting and emergency systems before a building can be occupied.

Which projects require Civil Defense approval?

New buildings, fit-outs, warehouses, industrial units and any premises with fire detection or suppression systems require DCD approval. Approval is needed at design stage and again after installation through inspection and the final NOC.

How do you help secure Civil Defense approval?

We prepare fire-protection drawings, submit them through the DCD portal, coordinate with approved contractors and arrange the final inspection. Our team ensures alarm, sprinkler and emergency systems meet code so the fire NOC is issued smoothly.
