Swimming Pool Approval


Building a swimming pool in Dubai requires adherence to specific rules and regulations, including obtaining Swimming Pool Approval. If you're looking to secure a construction permit quickly from the Dubai Municipality, simply reach out to the Dubai Approval Team for assistance.

  • Swimming Pool Approval

The Dubai Municipality (DM) has established specific guidelines and requirements that must be followed to obtain a construction permit for a swimming pool. Whether you're planning to build a private pool on your property or a public swimming facility, it's essential to follow the correct procedure to prevent the rejection of your application. Heres an overview of the steps involved in obtaining swimming pool approval in Dubai:

To apply for swimming pool approval in Dubai, the applicant must submit an application through the Dubai Municipality (DM) online portal, specifying whether the pool is private or public. After the application and supporting documents are submitted, a payment must be made according to the type of approval requested. A common concern is understanding the required documents and guidelines to ensure the application is not rejected. Heres a detailed look at the necessary documents and the key guidelines to follow during the process:

Required Documents

To receive approval for constructing a pool, the following documents are essential:

  • Site Plan
  • Site Setting Plan
  • Swimming Pool Detailed Plan
  • Cross-Sectional Details of the Pool with slope specifications
  • Plumbing Layout Plan
  • Electrical Layout
  • Filtration System Details
  • Outlet Drainage System
  • Details of Ladder, Skimmer, Lint & Hair Strainer, and Diving Board
  • Inlet Details
  • Lighting System
  • Backwash Water Disposal Plan

The pool design should clearly outline methods of filtration, water circulation, water disinfection, and safety & hygiene measures.

wimming Pool Design Plan

Guidelines for Pool Construction

Below are the important guidelines for pool construction that should be followed:

  • For pools with a depth greater than 0.6 meters, corrosion-resistant ladders and slip-free steps must be provided.
  • In larger pools, steps should be added every 30 meters.
  • Public pools must have toilet and changing room facilities.
  • If the pool will be used at night, ensure proper lighting underwater or overhead is installed to illuminate the entire pool.
  • Markings for pool depth should be clearly displayed on the walls and deck.
  • The pool deck should cover an area at least equal to or greater than the pool area and have a non-slip surface. The deck should also have a minimum slope of 1 in 40 away from the poolside with proper drainage to prevent waterlogging.
  • The pool should have an effective outlet drainage system, along with necessary strainers and disinfecting equipment.

Ensure that all construction plans meet the above requirements and safety standards. For professional guidance and a smooth approval process, consider consulting an expert in Dubai's pool construction regulations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is swimming pool approval?

Swimming pool approval is the permit required from Dubai Municipality or the relevant developer and authority to build a residential or commercial pool. It confirms the pool's structural, MEP and safety design meet code.

Which pools require approval?

New villa pools, community and hotel pools and major pool modifications require approval covering structural design, water treatment, electrical safety and barrier requirements before construction.

How do you handle swimming pool approval?

We prepare pool structural and MEP drawings, secure any developer NOC and submit to the authority, managing reviews and inspections until the construction permit and completion approval are issued.
