Spa Approval


For relaxation and leisure, many people prefer visiting spas in Dubai. If you're looking to start a spa business in the city, obtaining Spa Approval is a necessary step. We are here to assist you in securing quick and hassle-free approval for your spa business in Dubai.

Example of a spa setup for your business

1. Choosing the Type of Spa

First, decide on the type of spa you wish to operate. Will it be a traditional spa, a hair salon, or a facility offering additional beauty services like makeup, manicures, or skin treatments? You also need to consider your target audience a mens or womens spa, or one that serves both.

2. Business Setup Considerations

Next, determine whether you want to build your spa from scratch, purchase a ready-made space, or invest in a franchise. Also, think about the legal structure of the business do you wish to be the sole owner or enter into a partnership?

3. Essential Licenses and Permits

You must obtain all required licenses, including a basic business license, a no-objection certificate (NOC), and additional health and safety certifications. Documentation typically includes:

  • Location and site plans
  • Design and engineering plans
  • Electrical, plumbing, and ventilation system details
  • Health and safety certificates for staff
  • A valid Dubai Health Authority (DHA) License for all employees

4. Dubai Spa Approval Regulations

To meet Dubai's health and safety standards, certain regulations must be followed:

  • Approval from the Dubai Municipalitys planning department for the spas location.
  • A signboard must be displayed outside the facility.
  • Proper lighting, sanitary furniture, and equipment are mandatory.
  • Dressing areas should meet specific size requirements: at least 3.0 meters wide and 3.5 meters long.
  • The ceiling must be at least 2.3 meters high.
  • Different areas should be designated for various services like manicures, pedicures, hair removal, and henna application.
  • A wash basin must be located near facial treatment areas.
  • Separate handwashing stations are required in hair cutting lounges and facial treatment rooms.
  • Rooms should be no smaller than 2x3 meters and must have permanent partitions (no temporary curtains or accordions).
  • Cabinets and drawers are necessary for storing products like cosmetics and towels.
  • Fireproof materials must be used for hair removal preparations.
  • A water heater is mandatory for the spa.
  • All personal care products and cosmetics must be registered with the Dubai Municipalitys Consumer Product Safety Section.
  • DHA approval is required for the use of machines or equipment in the spa.

5. Additional Permissions for Residential Spas

If you plan to operate a spa at a residential location, you will need permission from the Dubai Economic Development Department.

Ensure all regulations and requirements are met to avoid delays in the approval process. For expert guidance, consult with a professional service to streamline the approval journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is spa approval in Dubai?

Spa approval covers the health, layout and licensing approvals required to operate a spa, including Dubai Municipality health-section and DHA-related requirements. It confirms the facility meets hygiene and safety standards.

Which spa projects need approval?

New spas, wellness centres and spa fit-outs within hotels or standalone premises require approval covering treatment-room layout, ventilation, drainage and hygiene before licensing.

How do you assist with spa approval?

We prepare compliant spa layouts and MEP drawings, submit to the relevant health and licensing authorities, and coordinate inspections so the facility meets all spa operating standards.
