Why do you require RTA Permit and Approval in Dubai?

The RTA was established by the Dubai Government with the vision of providing state-of-the-art, modern, and sophisticated road transport infrastructure. The RTA plays a significant role in ensuring the efficient monitoring and development of transportation, roads, and traffic in Dubai. Some of the key responsibilities of the RTA include:

  • Driving licenses and appointments of merchants
  • Parking services and toll (Salik) operations
  • Vehicle testing and registration
  • Water taxi booking services
  • Trade permit issuance and NOC services

Businesses related to activities such as private taxis, auto rentals, sale of vehicles, modification workshops, garages, and more require approval or NOC from RTA before obtaining a license from DED or other licensing authorities.

Getting Permit for Construction

We assist in obtaining the **Gate Level Permit** for construction in Dubai, which is required for approval before starting building work. The gate level is essentially the relative height of the building's entrance compared to the road level. Private building owners, consultants, contractors, or government agencies can apply for this permit.

Documents Required:

  • Effective Plot Plan
  • Topographic survey around the plot
  • DM Form for gate level requested by RTA

We help you get the Landscaping Permit

The **Landscaping Permit** allows the addition of soft landscaping areas along the roads without planting trees. This permit can be applied by individuals or companies.

Documents Required:

  • Effective plan from Dubai Municipality
  • Design of the landscaping area
  • Site photographs

We help you with the Supplementary Signs Permit

This permit is for placing supplementary signs that guide commuters to cultural, touristic, commercial, and other landmarks in Dubai. Government organizations and private companies with valid RTA permits are eligible for this approval.

Documents Required:

  • Effective plan from Dubai Municipality
  • Valid trade license
  • Effective plan specifying proposed locations for sign installation
  • Additional documents requested by the concerned department

NOC for Infrastructure

We help you obtain several NOCs under infrastructure-related services. These include:

  • NOC for Information
  • NOC for Construction Work
  • NOC for Trial Trenches Execution
  • NOC for Special Infrastructure Work
  • NOC for Certifying As-Built Drawings

Getting Permit for Outdoor Road Advertising

We assist with obtaining **temporary outdoor advertising permits** for special occasions, such as openings. Advertising companies can apply for this permit to install temporary promotional boards on roads.

Documents Required:

  • Valid trade license for the advertiser and the advertising company
  • Letter from the advertiser to RTA specifying the advertisement purpose and authorization
  • Letter from the advertising company detailing the proposed ad and responsibilities
  • Proposed advertisement design
  • Affection plan showing the proposed advertisement location

Renewal of Outdoor Advertising Permit

We help renew **temporary outdoor advertising permits** for special events. If you're an advertising company, you can apply for the renewal.

Documents Required:

  • Copy of valid trade license for the requesting party and the advertising company
  • Letter from the advertiser to RTA specifying the advertisement purpose and authorization
  • Letter from the advertising company detailing the proposed ad and responsibilities
  • Proposed advertisement design
  • Affection plan showing the proposed location
  • Previous advertising permit

Cancellation of Outdoor Advertising Permit

We help cancel the approval to install temporary advertising on roads for special occasions. If you have a valid permit, we assist you in this process.

Documents Required:

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RTA permit and approval?

RTA permit and approval is issued by Dubai's Roads and Transport Authority for works affecting roads, access, parking and traffic. It is required for road openings, access modifications and traffic diversions.

Which works require RTA approval?

New site access, road openings for utilities, parking layouts, traffic-diversion schemes and works near RTA infrastructure require RTA permits covering safety, traffic management and reinstatement.

How do you assist with RTA approvals?

We prepare access, parking and traffic drawings, submit applications and NOC requests through the RTA system, and coordinate approvals and inspections until the permit is issued.
