Property snagging is a thorough inspection process designed to identify any defects or necessary repairs in a property that the contractor or developer may have missed. The goal of snagging inspections in Dubai is to protect property investors and buyers from having to bear the costs and responsibility of fixing issues that should have been addressed before the property was handed over.

Understanding the Importance of Property Snagging and Inspection in Dubai

Snagging and inspection are crucial steps when receiving your property to ensure it's in perfect condition. This process involves having an independent engineering professional review the workmanship and finishes handed over by the contractor. This ensures that the work you are paying for meets the highest standards and minimizes potential issues in the future, giving you peace of mind.

5 Reasons Why Property Inspection is Needed

  1. Specialized Tools: Professional property inspectors use advanced tools such as thermographic imaging to identify hidden defects and areas that may not meet finishing standards.
  2. Expert Eyes: While some aesthetic errors are obvious, only a keen, experienced professional can detect defects in areas such as ceilings, AC systems, water pressure, and piping.
  3. Safety and Compliance: Inspectors can detect health hazards, safety issues (such as lift threats), and fire safety concerns that may not be immediately visible to the naked eye.
  4. Documented Snagging Report: A thorough, categorized inspection report provides a reference document that helps landlords ensure the developer rectifies all defects.
  5. Developer Obligation: By law, developers are required to fix any property defects before handing over the property, and a snagging report ensures compliance with this obligation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is property snagging and inspection?

Property snagging is a detailed inspection of a new or handed-over property to identify defects in finishes, MEP and workmanship before completion or handover. It produces a documented snag list for rectification.

When should I get a snagging inspection?

Before accepting handover of a new villa or apartment, or before the defects-liability period ends, a snagging inspection helps ensure the developer corrects faults at their cost.

What does your snagging service include?

Our inspectors check architectural finishes, doors, plumbing, electrical, HVAC and waterproofing, then provide a detailed report with photographs so defects can be rectified before you take possession.
