What is Infrastructure Design Approval in Dubai?

Infrastructure design approval is the authority endorsement of infrastructure designs confirming compliance with Dubai's technical standards. In Dubai this includes the NOC for Infrastructure Design and related NOCs from RTA and Dubai Municipality, required before infrastructure construction.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is infrastructure design approval?

Infrastructure design approval covers the engineering and authority sign-off for utilities and external works such as roads, drainage, water, electricity and telecom networks serving a development. It is required before infrastructure construction.

Which projects need infrastructure approval?

Master developments, plots and communities requiring new roads, sewerage, stormwater, water and power networks need infrastructure design approval from DEWA, RTA, the municipality and relevant utility authorities.

How do you handle infrastructure design approval?

We prepare coordinated infrastructure drawings and submit to each utility and authority, managing multi-agency reviews and NOCs until the full infrastructure design is approved for construction.
