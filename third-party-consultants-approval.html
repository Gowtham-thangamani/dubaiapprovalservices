Frequently Asked Questions

What is third party consultant approval?

Third party consultant approval is an independent review and certification of designs, structures or systems by an authority-approved specialist consultant. It is required for complex, high-rise or specialised works to verify compliance and safety.

When is a third party consultant required?

Authorities require third party review for items such as facade engineering, structural peer review, fire and life-safety systems and specialised structures, providing independent verification before approval.

How do you arrange third party consultant approval?

We coordinate with approved third party consultants, compile the design package for review, and manage the certification process so the independent approval is obtained efficiently for your project.
