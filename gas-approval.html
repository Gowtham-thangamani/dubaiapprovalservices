Gas Approval


Get professional gas approval services in Dubai for LPG installations, natural gas connections, and gas safety compliance. Our expert team ensures your gas systems meet all safety standards and regulatory requirements.

Gas approval is a critical requirement for any establishment in Dubai that uses LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas) or natural gas for cooking, heating, or industrial purposes. Whether you're opening a restaurant, caf, commercial kitchen, or industrial facility, obtaining gas approval from the relevant authorities is mandatory to ensure safety compliance. The Dubai Approvals Team specializes in handling all aspects of gas approvals, from initial design to final inspection and certification.

What is Gas Approval?

Gas approval in Dubai involves obtaining permits and certifications for the installation, modification, or use of gas systems in commercial and residential properties. This includes LPG cylinder installations, centralized LPG systems, natural gas connections from DEWA (Dubai Electricity and Water Authority), and industrial gas installations. The approval process ensures that all gas installations comply with safety standards set by Dubai Civil Defense, DEWA, and other regulatory authorities to prevent accidents and ensure public safety.

Why is Gas Approval Important?

Gas installations pose inherent safety risks if not properly designed, installed, and maintained. Dubai's stringent gas safety regulations are designed to protect lives and property. Non-compliance with gas safety requirements can result in serious consequences including fires, explosions, injuries, and legal penalties. Proper gas approval ensures:

  • Safe installation of gas equipment and piping
  • Adequate ventilation and safety measures
  • Proper gas leak detection systems
  • Compliance with fire safety requirements
  • Protection of occupants and neighboring properties

Documents Required for Gas Approval

The documentation requirements vary based on the type of gas installation and the property location. Our team will guide you through the specific requirements for your project.

Required Documents:

  1. Valid Trade License
  2. Tenancy Contract or Title Deed
  3. NOC from Building Management
  4. Approved architectural drawings
  5. Civil Defense approved fire safety drawings
  6. Kitchen layout with equipment specifications
  7. Gas equipment technical specifications
  8. Ventilation calculations and drawings
  9. Passport copies of authorized signatories

Required Technical Drawings:

  1. Gas piping layout with pipe sizes
  2. LPG cylinder room layout and details
  3. Gas meter location plan
  4. Kitchen exhaust hood layout
  5. Ventilation system layout
  6. Gas leak detector locations
  7. Emergency shut-off valve locations
  8. Fire suppression system layout (for kitchens)
  9. Gas equipment connection details
  10. Isometric drawings of gas piping

Frequently Asked Questions

What is gas approval in Dubai?

Gas approval certifies that LPG or piped-gas installations comply with Dubai Civil Defense and authority safety standards. It is required for restaurants, kitchens, industrial units and buildings using gas systems.

Which projects require gas approval?

Restaurant and kitchen gas lines, central gas systems, industrial gas installations and gas-bank works require approval covering design, materials and safety distances before commissioning.

How do you obtain gas approval?

We prepare gas system drawings, submit them to the relevant authority and approved gas suppliers, and coordinate inspection and testing until the installation is certified safe and approved for use.
