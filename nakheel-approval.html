How to Get Nakheel Approvals

Our team of specialists is here to guide you through every step of the Nakheel approval process. With our expert assistance, securing a lease for property within Nakheel has never been more straightforward. Below are some of the key documents you will need to obtain leasing approval from Nakheel:

  1. Passport Copy: A copy of the passport along with the visa page is required.
  2. Employment Letter or Work Permit: An employment letter or work permit is also necessary.
  3. Emirates ID Card: A copy of the Emirates ID card must be submitted.
  4. Contact Information: Provide contact details for communication.
  5. Legal Tenant Presence: Confirmation of the presence of a legal tenant is required.
  6. Company Lease: A copy of the company lease agreement should be provided.
  7. Letter of Confirmation: A letter of confirmation from the relevant authority is needed.
  8. Valid Trade License: A copy of the valid trade license must be submitted.
  9. Occupants' Passport Copy: A copy of the passport of all occupants is required.
  10. Contact Details: Provide detailed contact information for all involved parties.

You will also need to provide a rental cheque, which must be issued by a legally recognized tenant.

The initial rent payment along with the security deposit should be settled either in cash or through a manager's cheque.

A DEWA (Dubai Electricity and Water Authority) deposit should also be paid in advance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Nakheel approval?

Nakheel approval is the developer NOC required for properties in Nakheel communities such as Palm Jumeirah, Jumeirah Islands and Jumeirah Village. It is needed before modifications, fit-outs or construction works are carried out.

Which works need Nakheel approval?

Villa extensions, swimming pools, landscaping, fit-outs and structural changes within Nakheel-managed communities require Nakheel's developer approval, frequently alongside Trakhees or Dubai Municipality permits. We manage the full set.

How do you help with Nakheel approvals?

We prepare compliant drawings, submit to Nakheel's approvals team, and handle reviews and inspections until the NOC is issued, ensuring your works follow community design and engineering standards.
