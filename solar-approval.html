The Dubai government is committed to enhancing clean energy infrastructure to reduce reliance on non-renewable power sources. Residents are encouraged to install photovoltaic (PV) panels, which require mandatory approval from the Dubai Municipality. These panels, arranged in strings, are connected to an inverter by certified professionals authorized to work with solar systems in Dubai. The inverter converts the direct current (DC) produced by the PV panels into alternating current (AC). This electricity is then fed into the building's main distribution board. With Dubai Municipality solar approval, homeowners can use the electricity generated by their panels, reducing dependence on the grid until sunset. Any excess energy produced is credited to the users account.

Process of Solar Approval in Dubai

In Dubai, obtaining solar approval is crucial for ensuring that solar systems are installed according to regulations and are safely connected to the power grid. The process involves several stages, starting with obtaining the No Objection Certificate (NOC) and completing various approval requirements before connection to the grid. Below, we outline the key steps in the solar approval process in Dubai:

Initial Approval & Certification

  1. No Objection Certificate (NOC): A vital certificate required before applying for a solar permit to connect PV panels to the grid.
  2. Solar Design Approval: Engineers must submit a draft design of the solar array to Dubai Municipality for approval before construction.
  3. Connection Charges: Contractors are required to pay for connection charges through the online portal for solar consultants and contractors.

Final Stages: Inspection & Connection

  • Site Inspection: After paying connection charges, the authority will conduct an inspection of the site before proceeding with the grid connection.
  • Performance Test Report: Depending on the nature of the installation, a performance test report may be required to confirm the system's functionality before approval.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is solar approval in Dubai?

Solar approval covers the DEWA Shams Dubai connection and NOCs required to install rooftop solar photovoltaic systems and feed power into the grid. It is required for grid-connected solar installations in Dubai.

Which projects need solar approval?

Residential, commercial and industrial rooftop solar PV systems require solar approval under DEWA's Shams Dubai initiative, covering design, electrical connection and net-metering before energising.

How do you assist with solar approval?

We prepare solar PV and single-line drawings, submit through DEWA's Shams Dubai programme with an approved enrolled contractor, and coordinate inspection and meter installation until the system is connected.
