Documents to be Submitted for DCCA Approval

The process of obtaining approval from the Dubai Creative Clusters Authority (DCCA) involves submitting several documents and undergoing inspections at various stages of the project. Below are some essential documents and steps required at different stages:

For Pre-Construction

During the pre-construction phase, several documents and inspections are required to obtain DCCA approval. These include:

  • Soil investigation
  • Soil investigation inspection
  • Soil report review
  • Design revision
  • Traffic impact study
  • MEP preliminary design
  • Infrastructure work permit
  • Preliminary building permit
  • Final building permits
For Construction

For the construction stage, you will need to submit the following documents:

  • Shoring completion certificate
  • Piling completion certificate
  • Completion of structural inspection
  • Dewatering NOC
  • Final design review - post tension
  • Design revision
  • Road closure/diversion approvals
  • Project safety officer approval
  • Infrastructure work completion certificate
For Post-Construction

After construction, these documents are required for final approval:

  • Fit-out permit
  • Fit-out permit initiation
  • Design revision
  • Facility management service provider details

Frequently Asked Questions

What is TECOM and DCCA approval?

TECOM and DCCA approval is issued by the authorities governing Dubai's business communities such as Media City, Internet City and Knowledge Park, now under the Dubai Creative Clusters Authority. It is required for fit-out and building works in those zones.

Which works require TECOM/DCCA approval?

Office and retail fit-outs, signage, electrical and mechanical works and modifications within TECOM/DCCA-managed communities require approval covering planning, building and life-safety compliance.

How do you assist with TECOM and DCCA approvals?

We prepare compliant fit-out and building drawings, submit through the authority's system, and manage reviews and inspections until the fit-out permit and completion approvals are issued.
