Guidelines for signage approval

Our specialists emphasize that obtaining signboard approval in Dubai depends on several factors, including the size, location, design, and message of the signage. Ensuring safety is a priority, particularly when dealing with large structures like hoarding boards. Approval for hoarding boards cannot be granted unless proper safety measures are implemented to guarantee secure installation. Additionally, signage must align with UAE regulations, which require that content reflects public decency and adheres to local cultural values. Signage approval is only granted if the name displayed matches the one registered on the companys DMCC Trade License. To prevent disturbances, especially regarding light pollution, signs that cause disruption to nearby properties will not be approved. Furthermore, the materials used must be durable and fire-resistant, with all electrical components fully insulated and concealed from public view. The application for signage approval must detail the type of materials, lighting, and dimensions used. Along with the signage application, a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the building owner or developer is required, as well as a Permit to Work. Temporary signage, such as Opening Soon signs, also needs to be approved. If the business caters to a specific gender, this must be clearly stated on the sign as well.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is signage approval in Dubai?

Signage approval is the permit required from Dubai Municipality or the relevant free zone and developer to install shop, building or directional signage. It confirms the sign meets size, safety and design regulations.

Which signage needs approval?

Shopfront signs, building name boards, illuminated and projecting signs and hoardings require signage approval covering dimensions, structural fixing, electrical safety and community guidelines before installation.

How do you help with signage approval?

We prepare signage drawings and structural details, secure any developer NOC and submit to the authority, managing reviews and inspection until the signage permit is issued.
