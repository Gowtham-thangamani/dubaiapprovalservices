How to Get Shisha Cafe Approval in Dubai

To begin the process of setting up a Shisha caf in Dubai, you will first need to obtain initial approval by submitting a Shisha Permit Service request, followed by a trade name reservation. You will also need to sign agreements at the Notary Public in Dubai, provide an attested tenancy contract for the premises, and secure approval from the Dubai Municipality. Passport copies of all partners involved in the business are required when submitting these documents. Once the paperwork is submitted, Dubai Municipality will conduct an inspection of the premises and provide final approval for the Shisha permit. Additionally, they will guide you on maintaining compliance with the regulations to ensure smooth operations and avoid any future licensing issues.

Required Documents for Shisha Caf Approval in Dubai:

To obtain approval for your Shisha bar, several documents are required to ensure that all guidelines are met for Shisha permission in Dubai. Heres a quick overview of the necessary documents:

  • Location plan, design, and engineering plans
  • Detailed electrical, plumbing, lighting, and ventilation system plans
  • All other documents as required by the Dubai Municipality for a Shisha caf license

Ensuring all these documents are submitted correctly will streamline the approval process and help you avoid any delays or complications.

Guidelines to Get the Shisha Cafe License Approval in Dubai:

Dubai Municipality has set several specific regulations for obtaining a Shisha permit that applicants must be aware of. Familiarity with these guidelines is essential to ensure a smooth application process:

  1. The Shisha establishment must be located in a commercial building along a main road. The owner must also provide a No Objection Certificate (NOC) for serving Shisha on the premises.
  2. The minimum indoor area for the Shisha facility should be at least 200 square meters, with the caf bar occupying a space of 150 square meters.
  3. There must be a minimum distance of 150 meters between the Shisha caf and any surrounding residential buildings, apartments, or other structures.
  4. The distance from the Shisha facility to nearby schools and nurseries must also be at least 150 meters. Similarly, the facility must be 150 meters away from mosques and other places of worship.
  5. The Shisha caf should face the main street, with a well-designed ventilation system. Additionally, the location must be approved by local authorities for its proximity to the main road.
  6. A separate outdoor entrance must be part of the facilitys basic design. The service area must be isolated from the rest of the building, ensuring privacy and safety.
  7. The entrance to the Shisha bar should be set back at least 7.5 meters from the buildings main entrance.
  8. Its mandatory for cafes offering Shisha to have at least three non-smoking rooms, and the Shisha area should be distinct from these. Additionally, the Shisha lounge should have a ceiling height of at least 3 meters.

Only if you meet all these conditions and submit the correct documentation will your Shisha caf permit application be considered successfully. Frequently Asked Questions

What is a shisha cafe license in Dubai?

A shisha cafe license is the combined permit allowing a cafe or restaurant to serve shisha, covering Dubai Municipality, location and ventilation conditions. It confirms the premises meet shisha-specific health and distance rules.

What are the requirements for a shisha license?

Requirements include an approved location at the required distance from residential and sensitive sites, compliant ventilation and outdoor or designated serving areas, plus municipality and economic-department approvals.
