Effective storm water management protects roads, buildings and infrastructure from flooding. Our Dubai team designs efficient storm water drainage networks to approved standards, combining hydraulic analysis with practical routing and full coordination with Dubai Municipality and RTA.

From catchment analysis to approved drawings, we make storm water design straightforward — handling hydraulic studies, network routing and authority submissions so your project proceeds without delay.

What does Storm Water Network Design involve?

Storm water network design is the engineering of drainage systems that collect, convey and safely discharge rainfall runoff. It covers catchment analysis, hydraulic studies, pipe and channel sizing, and optimal route planning in line with Dubai Municipality standards and building codes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is storm water network design?

Storm water network design covers the drainage system that collects and conveys rainwater and surface runoff, including pipes, gullies, soakaways and outfalls. It protects developments from flooding and meets authority standards.

Which projects need storm water design?

Plots, roads and master developments require storm water network design to manage runoff, sized according to municipality and drainage-authority criteria before infrastructure construction.

How do you handle storm water network design?

We carry out drainage calculations and design the network of pipes, catch basins and discharge points, then submit to the relevant authority and manage approvals through to sanction.
