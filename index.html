Frequently Asked Questions

What is Dubai Approval and why do I need it?

Dubai Approval refers to the mandatory government and authority permits required before starting any construction, fit-out, or renovation project in Dubai. Every project must obtain approvals from relevant authorities such as Dubai Municipality, Civil Defense, DEWA, and developer-specific bodies like Emaar or Nakheel. Without these approvals, your project cannot legally proceed and may face fines or demolition orders.

How long does it take to get a Dubai approval?

The timeline for Dubai approval varies depending on the type of project and authority involved. Simple fit-out approvals may take 2-4 weeks, while complex construction projects requiring multiple authority approvals can take 4-12 weeks. Our team at Dubai Approval Services expedites the process by ensuring all documentation is complete and compliant from the first submission, minimizing rejections and delays.

Which authorities handle Dubai approvals?

Dubai has multiple approval authorities including Dubai Municipality (DM), Dubai Civil Defense (DCD), DEWA for electricity and water, Roads and Transport Authority (RTA), Trakhees for free zone areas, and developer-specific bodies such as Emaar, Nakheel, Dubai South, DIEZ, JAFZA, DHCC, and TECOM. Each authority has specific requirements and submission processes. Dubai Approval Services handles all 29+ authorities on your behalf.

How much does Dubai approval cost?

Dubai approval costs depend on the project scope, type of approval, and number of authorities involved. We offer competitive and transparent pricing with no hidden fees. Contact us at +971 4 285 1590 or email info@dubaiapprovalservices.com for a free quotation tailored to your specific project requirements.

Can you handle approvals for villa renovation in Dubai?

Yes, Dubai Approval Services specializes in villa renovation approvals including structural modifications, extensions, swimming pool construction, and interior fit-outs. We handle the complete process from initial drawings and NOC from the developer to Dubai Municipality approval and Civil Defense clearance, ensuring your renovation project is fully compliant with Dubai building codes.
