Parking Design


Dubai Approvals Team provides professional parking design services in Dubai, delivering efficient, compliant car park layouts and the required RTA and authority approvals.

  • Home
  • Parking Design

Well-designed parking improves access, safety and the value of any development. We design efficient car park layouts that maximise capacity, ensure smooth circulation, and comply with RTA Dubai and Dubai Municipality parking standards.

From parking provision studies to approved layouts, we handle parking design and the authority approvals that come with it.

What does Parking Design involve?

Parking design covers the layout and engineering of surface, basement and multi-storey car parks. It includes parking provision calculations, bay and aisle geometry, circulation and ramp design, accessibility, and compliance with Dubai parking and transport standards.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does parking design involve?

Parking design covers the layout, circulation, ramps, dimensions and capacity of parking areas to meet Dubai Municipality and RTA standards. It ensures safe, efficient and code-compliant parking for a development.

Why is approved parking design important?

Authorities require parking provision that meets minimum space counts, aisle widths, ramp gradients and accessibility rules. Compliant parking design is essential for the overall building permit and traffic approval.

How do you handle parking design and approval?

We prepare parking layout and ramp drawings coordinated with the building and RTA requirements, then support submission and corrections until the parking design is approved.
