Dubai Healthcare City (DHCC) is the world's largest healthcare free zone, dedicated to providing world-class medical and wellness services. Established in 2002, DHCC spans over 4.1 million square feet and houses more than 170 healthcare facilities. Whether you're planning to set up a clinic, hospital, medical center, pharmacy, or wellness facility within DHCC, obtaining the proper approvals from DHCC Authority (DHCA) is essential for compliance and successful operations.

What is DHCC?

Dubai Healthcare City (DHCC) is a dedicated healthcare free zone that provides a comprehensive ecosystem for medical, wellness, and educational services. DHCC is regulated by the Dubai Healthcare City Authority (DHCA), which ensures that all facilities meet international healthcare standards. The free zone offers numerous benefits including 100% foreign ownership, zero corporate taxes, full repatriation of profits, and access to a skilled medical workforce. DHCC is home to hospitals, specialty clinics, diagnostic centers, pharmacies, and wellness facilities serving patients from across the region.

Why Choose DHCC Approval Services?

Operating a healthcare facility within DHCC requires compliance with strict regulatory standards set by the Dubai Healthcare City Authority. These regulations ensure patient safety, quality of care, and adherence to international healthcare practices. The approval process involves multiple stages including facility design approval, fit-out permits, and operational licenses.

The Dubai Approvals Team provides comprehensive DHCC approval services to help healthcare providers navigate the regulatory requirements efficiently. Our expertise covers all aspects of the approval process, from initial facility design to final operational approvals.

Documents Required for DHCC Approval

Healthcare facilities in DHCC must meet specific design and operational requirements. Our team will guide you through the documentation process to ensure compliance with DHCA regulations.

Required Documents:

  1. DHCC Trade License (or application)
  2. Tenancy Contract or Lease Agreement
  3. NOC from Building Management
  4. Healthcare facility license application
  5. Architectural drawings (AutoCAD format)
  6. MEP drawings and specifications
  7. Medical gas system layouts (if applicable)
  8. Radiation protection plans (if applicable)
  9. Fire and safety layout plans
  10. Company registration documents
  11. Professional qualifications of medical staff

Required Drawings for Healthcare Facilities:

  1. Detailed floor layout with room specifications
  2. Patient flow diagrams
  3. Clean and dirty utility room layouts
  4. Medical gas piping layout
  5. HVAC layout with pressure differentials
  6. Electrical layout with emergency power
  7. Fire alarm and suppression system layout
  8. Nurse call system layout
  9. Radiation shielding details (if applicable)
  10. Infection control zone designations

Frequently Asked Questions

What is DHCC approval?

DHCC approval is issued by Dubai Healthcare City Authority for healthcare and commercial premises within the Dubai Healthcare City free zone. It covers building fit-out, engineering and facility licensing inside DHCC.

Which projects require DHCC approval?

Clinics, medical centres, pharmacies and commercial fit-outs located in Dubai Healthcare City require DHCC approval, which addresses both construction compliance and healthcare facility standards. We confirm the exact requirements.

How do you handle DHCC submissions?

We prepare DHCC-compliant drawings, submit through the authority's system and manage reviews and inspections until the fit-out permit and operational approvals are granted.
