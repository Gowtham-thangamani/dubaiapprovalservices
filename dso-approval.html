What is DSO?

Dubai Silicon Oasis (DSO) is a free trade zone established by the Dubai government in 2004, aimed at transforming the city into a global hub for high-tech industries. With a range of incentives and state-of-the-art facilities provided by the DSO Authority, the tech park has successfully attracted numerous high-tech companies from around the world. Covering an area of 7.2 square kilometers, DSO boasts world-class infrastructure, backed by significant government investment, making it one of the most attractive locations for business startups.

Submission of Infrastructure Request for Electricity and Water

The Infrastructure and Information Departments allow registered contractors to submit online applications for obtaining electricity and water infrastructure services. While the application process itself is free, the actual service comes with a fee. Securing DEWA approval can be a complex and time-consuming process, requiring the submission of multiple documents, which can often be overwhelming.

Submission of Fit-Out Application for Getting DSO Approvals

This service allows customers to acquire a fit-out power connection for their shops and offices, and it is provided at no charge.

Apply for a Connection to Electricity or Water

This service helps the customers get Electricity or Water connections. Accessing this service, customers can fill out a detailed application form through One Window System using their User ID and Password. This application will be reviewed by the DEWAs authority for giving confirmation of the Approval. Application service is fully free of cost while service is chargeable.

Building NOC:

This service is available at no cost for application, though there may be charges for the service itself. It allows registered contractors to obtain the necessary electricity and water No Objection Certificates (NOC) required for securing a building permit.

Documents required for initial DSO Approval

To begin the process, you must first submit a letter of intent. Additionally, a copy of the managers passport and the freehold/leasehold acknowledgment form are required. The completed application form must also be provided. Other necessary documents include a copy of the DED, unit title deed, and the office tenancy contract. Furthermore, you will need to demonstrate a minimum capital of AED 10,000 to establish a business in DSO.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is DSO approval?

DSO approval refers to the building and engineering permits issued by Dubai Silicon Oasis Authority for projects within the Dubai Silicon Oasis free zone. It is required for construction, fit-out and modification works in that community.

Which works require Dubai Silicon Oasis approval?

Office and retail fit-outs, new buildings, signage, electrical and mechanical works and any structural modifications inside Dubai Silicon Oasis require DSO approval. We identify the correct submissions for your scope.

How do you assist with DSO submissions?

We prepare DSO-compliant drawings, manage the submission and review process, and coordinate inspections until the building permit and completion certificate are issued by the authority.
