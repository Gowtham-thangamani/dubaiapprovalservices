Mezzanine Approval


Get professional mezzanine floor approval services in Dubai. Our expert team handles structural approvals, Dubai Municipality permits, and Civil Defense compliance for warehouses, retail spaces, and commercial facilities.

Mezzanine floors are an excellent solution for maximizing space utilization in warehouses, retail stores, offices, and industrial facilities. In Dubai, constructing a mezzanine floor requires proper approvals from various authorities including Dubai Municipality, Civil Defense, and free zone authorities (if applicable). The Dubai Approvals Team specializes in obtaining all necessary permits for mezzanine construction, ensuring your project complies with structural, safety, and regulatory requirements.

What is Mezzanine Approval?

Mezzanine approval is the process of obtaining official permits and certifications for constructing an intermediate floor between the main floors of a building. This typically involves structural engineering approval, Dubai Municipality permits, and Civil Defense clearances to ensure the mezzanine meets safety standards. Mezzanine floors are commonly used in warehouses for additional storage, in retail spaces for expanded display areas, and in offices for creating additional work areas without changing the building's external footprint.

Why is Mezzanine Approval Important?

Constructing a mezzanine floor without proper approvals is illegal in Dubai and can result in serious consequences including:

  • Fines and penalties from authorities
  • Forced removal of the unauthorized structure
  • Safety hazards due to improper structural design
  • Issues with trade license renewal
  • Problems when selling or leasing the property
  • Insurance claim rejections

Proper mezzanine approval ensures that your structure is safe, compliant, and legally recognized.

Documents Required for Mezzanine Approval

The approval process requires several documents and technical drawings. Our team will prepare and submit all necessary documentation on your behalf.

Required Documents:

  1. Valid Trade License
  2. Tenancy Contract or Title Deed
  3. NOC from Building Owner/Management
  4. Original building approved drawings
  5. Structural consultant registration documents
  6. Insurance certificate for structural work
  7. Passport copies of authorized signatories
  8. Power of Attorney (if applicable)

Required Technical Drawings:

  1. Structural layout plan with dimensions
  2. Steel structure details and specifications
  3. Column and beam connection details
  4. Foundation connection details
  5. Load calculations and structural analysis
  6. Floor plan showing mezzanine area
  7. Section drawings showing height clearances
  8. Staircase design and details
  9. Handrail and balustrade details
  10. Fire safety system layout
  11. Electrical and lighting layout
  12. Emergency exit and signage plan

Frequently Asked Questions

What is mezzanine approval?

Mezzanine approval is the permit required to add an intermediate floor within a building or warehouse. It confirms the mezzanine's structural design, loading, access and fire-safety comply with Dubai Municipality or free-zone codes.

Which mezzanine works need approval?

Adding a mezzanine in a warehouse, showroom, shop or office requires approval covering structural calculations, staircases, fire escapes and load limits before construction. Free-zone properties need the zone authority's approval.

How do you handle mezzanine approval?

We prepare structural and architectural drawings with load calculations, submit to the relevant authority and coordinate inspections until the mezzanine permit and completion approval are issued.
