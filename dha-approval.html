DHA Approval


Quality healthcare is a necessity for all. The Dubai Approvals Team will handle all the details for you and secure your DHA Approval swiftly.

As Dubais population continues to grow, the demand for healthcare professionals is also rising. The citys thriving economy creates a strong need for skilled workers, and each year, many doctors, nurses, and medical interns come to Dubai to advance their careers in healthcare. However, to practice medicine in the UAE, professionals must follow specific guidelines under DHA Approval to obtain their license. Unlike other countries, the UAE has different licenses for healthcare practitioners. There are four separate licenses: MOH, DHCC, DHA, and HAAD. Each license is valid within its respective field and authority, particularly within the Dubai Healthcare Authority (DHA) framework. The DHA oversees licensing in the mainland area of Dubai, excluding the Dubai Healthcare City free zone. As the regulatory body, the DHA is responsible for operating and managing four government hospitals in the city.

To work as a healthcare professional in the mainland of Dubai, obtaining a DHA license is essential. The process can be complex, requiring extensive paperwork and accurate documentation, making it challenging without the right guidance. A reliable company specializing in license approvals can simplify this process. Healthcare professionals can apply for two types of DHA licenses: a Full License, for those meeting all the requirements, and a Provisional License, for those who dont yet meet the full license criteria but can work under the supervision of a specialist or consultant. The DHA issues licenses in three categories for doctors: General Practitioners, Specialists, and Consultants.

Getting Started with Setting Up a Clinic in Dubai

Setting up a clinic in Dubai requires obtaining the necessary approvals from the Dubai Health Authority (DHA). The process begins with the registration of a trade name, which involves submitting an online application to the Department of Economic Development (DED). Here are the initial steps to consider when starting this process:

Initial Requirements for DHA Approval

  1. No Objection Certificate (NOC): Obtain an NOC from the owner to assign the Medical Director of the clinic.
  2. Building Contract: You need a valid building contract for the clinic location.
  3. Healthcare Professionals List: Submit a list of healthcare professionals who will be working in your clinic.
  4. Valid Passport Copy: Provide a valid passport copy of the applicants and stakeholders. Keep in mind that visa requirements may change, so its important to stay updated with the latest regulations.

Understanding the DHA Approval Process

The DHA approval process is a step-by-step online procedure designed to ensure that healthcare services meet high standards of care. The approval can take up to two months, although in some cases, it may take longer (up to a year). The cost of the entire process is typically around AED 5000. You can choose to handle the process yourself or hire a professional service provider to guide you through the process.

Key Steps in DHA Approval

  • Layout Plan Submission: Present your clinic layout plan to the concerned authorities.
  • Dubai Municipality Approval: Attach a copy of the Dubai Municipality approval for your layout plan and the trade name reservation letter from DED.
  • Proposal Letters from Partners: If you have any business partners, provide proposal letters from them.
  • Accurate Documentation: Ensure that all documents are correctly submitted and meet the DHAs requirements.

Medical Fitness and Age Considerations

If any of your applicants or healthcare professionals are 65 years of age or older, a medical fitness test will be conducted as part of the approval process.

Final Thoughts

Once all requirements are met and the DHA approval process is complete, you will be ready to open your clinic in Dubai. Understanding the licensing process and ensuring that all documents are in order is crucial for a smooth and successful approval process. If you're unfamiliar with the process, its advisable to work with a service provider who can streamline the procedure for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is DHA approval?

DHA approval is the licensing and facility approval issued by the Dubai Health Authority for healthcare premises such as clinics, pharmacies and medical centres. It confirms the facility meets DHA design, safety and operational standards.

Which healthcare projects need DHA approval?

New clinics, dental centres, pharmacies, laboratories and medical fit-outs require DHA approval covering layout, infection control and medical-gas and safety requirements before they can operate.

How do you assist with DHA approval?

We prepare DHA-compliant medical facility drawings, submit the application and coordinate inspections, ensuring the layout and engineering services meet Dubai Health Authority requirements for licensing.
