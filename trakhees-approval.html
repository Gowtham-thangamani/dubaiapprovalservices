What is Trakhees Approval?

Trakhees is dedicated to supporting the Dubai Strategic Plan 2021, driving the emirate's continuous progress in innovative and sustainable economic development. This vision is pivotal in advancing the facilities, services, and performance of the Ports, Customs, and Free Zone Corporation. Trakhees offers valuable guidance on strategic plans that incorporate the latest technological advancements, helping to align both current and future objectives. Their approval regulations and standards are designed to foster creativity and innovation, enhancing the quality of public services, including civil engineering solutions.

Process of Trakhees Approval Service

Trakhees provides a comprehensive set of approval services aimed at ensuring that all developments within Dubai adhere to the highest standards. Whether you are planning a new construction or need approval for modifications to an existing structure, Trakhees plays a vital role in making the process smooth and efficient. Below, we outline the various stages and services involved in the Trakhees approval process:

Planning Services

  • NOC for Master Plans: Approval required for overall planning of the project.
  • Site Plan and Lease Drawings: Submission of site plans and lease agreement drawings for approval.
  • NOC for General and Service Authority: Getting approval from the relevant authorities.
  • Demarcation Certificates: Confirmation of boundary and land area.
  • Advertising NOC: Required if you plan to advertise on the property.
  • Plot or Right of Way: Legal confirmation of plot rights or access.
  • Obtaining or Renewal of Planning General NOC: General approval for planning and development.

Permit Services

  • Building Permit: Necessary for starting construction work.
  • Change of Consultant-Contractor Procedures: Required if there are any changes in the project team.
  • Concept Design Approval: Approval for initial design concepts before moving forward.
  • Consultant Registration: Ensures your consultants are registered and recognized.
  • Demolition and Removal NOC: Permission needed to demolish or remove structures.
  • Exceptional Study: Special study approvals for non-standard developments.
  • General NOC (Grading-leveling, Shaded Parking, Landscaping): Permits for land preparation and landscaping work.
  • Mobilization NOC: Approval for construction site setup.
  • New Building System Approval: Permits for new building systems and technologies.
  • Software Approval Procedure: For any software-related construction process approvals.
  • Suspension of Ongoing Works: Request for halting works due to unforeseen circumstances.

Modification Services

  • Modifications Permit: Required for any changes to the original design or structure.
  • Modification Completion Certificate: Confirmation that all modifications have been completed as per standards.

Inspection & Compliance Services

  • Structural Inspections: Ensuring the structure is up to code and safe.
  • Completion Certificate: Official approval confirming that the project is finished.
  • Site Status Inspections: Regular inspections to ensure compliance with regulations.
  • Construction Safety Inspections: Ensuring safety standards are met throughout the building process.
  • NOC for Night Construction Activities: Permission for construction work to take place at night.
  • 3rd Party Scaffold Training Providers: Registration and renewal for scaffold safety training providers.

Permit Section for Facilities

  • Green Building Permits: Registration and permits for environmentally friendly construction.
  • Facility Management Service Provider Registration: Ensuring proper facility management post-construction.

Trakhees Approval Regulations and Guidelines

Trakhees enforces regulations to ensure that developments in Dubai meet environmental sustainability standards. The guidelines cover various aspects of building regulations, from structural designs to construction materials and urban planning. Some of the key regulations include:

  • Building Design and Structural Requirements: Minimum standards for architectural and structural elements.
  • Construction Materials Quality Control: Ensuring the use of high-quality, compliant materials in construction.
  • Accessibility Regulations: Ensuring the building is accessible for people with disabilities.
  • Urban Planning and MEP Regulations: Compliance with Dubais urban planning and mechanical, electrical, and plumbing standards.
  • Concrete Testing Regulations: Ensuring concrete used in construction meets international standards.

By following Trakhees approval process and guidelines, you ensure that your project adheres to all legal, environmental, and safety standards, making it ready for the future of sustainable development in Dubai.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Trakhees approval?

Trakhees is the regulatory arm of Ports, Customs and Free Zone Corporation (PCFC) that issues building, engineering and licensing approvals within its jurisdiction, including areas such as Palm Jumeirah and other PCFC zones. Its approval is required for construction and fit-out works in those areas.

Which projects fall under Trakhees jurisdiction?

Developments within PCFC-controlled communities and free zones, including residential, commercial and industrial projects in areas like Palm Jumeirah, require Trakhees approval rather than Dubai Municipality. We confirm jurisdiction and route your submission correctly.

How do you assist with Trakhees submissions?

We prepare compliant architectural, structural and MEP drawings, submit through the Trakhees system and manage reviews, comments and inspections until the building permit and completion approvals are issued.
