Tent Approval


Celebrate the spirit of Ramadan or any special occasion with peace of mind. Dubai Approvals can help you secure tent approvals faster than anyone else.

Planning to set up temporary tents in Dubai during Ramadan to provide a space for people to gather and pray is a noble gesture. However, there are certain regulations to follow, including obtaining Ramadan tent approval, to avoid legal issues. Anyone planning to install Ramadan tents must secure the proper approvals, as outlined by the Dubai Municipality in a directive issued on May 1, 2018. This guideline specifies the various authorities from which approval must be obtained, such as the Dubai Municipality, Dubai Civil Defense, traffic authorities, and utility providers. Understanding the requirements for obtaining tent approvals is crucial. The following provides important information on the guidelines for setting up temporary tents during Ramadan.

Setting up temporary tents in Dubai, especially during Ramadan, requires obtaining necessary approvals as mandated by Law No. 16/2009, which regulates construction projects. This law prohibits the installation of temporary structures without proper permits, and violators may face fines and be responsible for removing the structure at their own expense. The approval process involves multiple authorities, including the Dubai Municipality, which ensures safety and security standards, the General Directorate of Civil Defence, which assesses safety factors, and utility providers like the Abu Dhabi Distribution Company, which handles electrical connections. Additionally, traffic authorities must approve the placement of tents in public areas to safeguard public safety and the community's well-being.

Understanding the Application Process for Temporary Tents in Dubai

If you plan to set up temporary tents in Dubai, such as for Ramadan, it is crucial to follow the right process to ensure all approvals are in place. The first step involves submitting an application to the Abu Dhabi City Municipality in the prescribed format. Along with your application, you must submit supporting documents and an undertaking to comply with the Municipalitys guidelines.

Required Documents for Tent Approvals

  1. Valid License: Ensure that you provide a valid license for your tent setup.
  2. Application Form or Permit Request: Submit the application form or permit request to the Municipality.
  3. Comprehensive Description of the Event: Provide a detailed description of the planned event that includes the tents setup and purpose.

One great thing to note is that the Municipality provides tent approvals as a complimentary service, so there are no fees associated with this approval process.

Essential Guidelines for Tent Setups

In addition to submitting the required documents, there are several guidelines that must be followed when constructing temporary tents:

  • Utility Line Protection: Ensure that the tents do not interfere with or cause damage to utility lines, streets, or sidewalks.
  • Avoid Obstruction: The tent should not block pedestrian movement, emergency exits, streets, entry points to buildings, or car parks.
  • No Private Property Usage: Tents should not be set up on private property without permission from the owner.

Be Ready for Inspections

Its important to note that inspectors assigned by the Municipality will verify that all the guidelines and requirements are being followed. Inspections will take place both before and after the setup of the tent to ensure compliance with the standards and specifications. Violations of the requirements will prevent you from obtaining the necessary approvals.

Final Thoughts

Obtaining the necessary approvals for temporary tents can ensure that your event, particularly during Ramadan, is a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone in the community. Make sure to follow all guidelines to ensure a smooth and successful approval process.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is tent approval?

Tent approval is the temporary-structure permit and Civil Defense NOC required to erect tents for events, weddings, exhibitions or Ramadan majlis in Dubai. It confirms the tent meets fire-safety and structural requirements.

When is tent approval required?

Any temporary tent used for events, hospitality or seasonal use requires approval covering fire-retardant materials, emergency exits, electrical safety and structural stability before installation.

How do you obtain tent approval?

We prepare tent layout and safety drawings, submit to Dubai Civil Defense and the relevant authority, and arrange the inspection so your temporary structure is approved for safe use.
